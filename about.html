About Me

Hello, I’m Orix. I’m an Applied Media Communications student, and this site is my “memory capsule”. A place to store the projects I have made as I learn more about video, photography, design, and audio.

Who I Am

I’m a media student who likes mixing different types of work: video, motion graphics, photography, and audio. I enjoy learning how all of those pieces come together to tell a story and create a specific mood.

Why I Made This Site

I wanted a place where I could look back and see how my work changes over time. Instead of only posting “perfect” finished pieces, this site shows the real progression class projects, experiments, and things I’m still figuring out. It’s meant to feel honest, not super polished.

What I Like Making

I’m especially drawn to darker, slightly creepy visuals, high-contrast lighting, and stylized color. I also like creating stories on live-action video.

Where I’m Heading

In the future, I’d like to keep creating work that blends storytelling with strong visuals whether that’s music videos, short films, motion graphics, or design. This site is the starting point, and I’ll keep updating it as I learn more and take on new projects.

Quick Facts

  • Based in Cincinnati, OH
  • Studying Applied Media Communications
  • Interested in video, photo, design & audio
  • Loves creepy / surreal visual styles
